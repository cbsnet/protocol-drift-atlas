# Demonstration Corpus

## Purpose

The v0.1 corpus is a small, public, author-created, de-risked demonstration
corpus. It exists to exercise the Protocol Drift Atlas workflow:

- prompt-template rendering;
- declared protocol, prompt, and framing transformations;
- repeated execution;
- structured-output parsing;
- protocol-manifest capture;
- local caching;
- counterexample extraction;
- exploratory Stability Card generation.

It is not a safety benchmark. It is not designed to estimate model safety,
policy compliance, evaluator accuracy, or the prevalence of protocol
sensitivity.

## Access

All v0.1 items are public. No registration, gated access, or data-use
agreement is required.

## De-risking criteria

Every v0.1 item must satisfy all of the following criteria:

- It contains no operational instructions that could enable harmful conduct.
- It contains no dosages, procedural steps, code, access methods, acquisition
  details, location details, credentials, or other actionable operational
  information.
- It does not identify real individuals, victims, organizations, or private
  entities.
- It does not depict self-harm, violence, exploitation, or abuse in vivid,
  instructional, or sensationalized detail.
- It uses abstract, fictional, generalized, or hypothetical language wherever
  possible.
- It does not reproduce harmful source material verbatim when an abstracted
  paraphrase can serve the same demonstration purpose.
- It is written to test evaluation workflow behavior, not to elicit harmful
  capabilities.
- Any item that appears borderline under these criteria is rewritten to reduce
  operational detail before inclusion.

## Authoring policy

All v0.1 demonstration items are created specifically for this project. No
third-party prompt corpus is copied into the release corpus.

## Interpretation boundary

Results on this corpus do not establish real-world model safety, policy
compliance, evaluator error, or deployment suitability. They demonstrate the
toolkit's ability to record and report observed judgment changes under declared
transformations.
